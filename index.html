Who I am

Building Africa's
deep-tech future

I'm Ibrahim, a 19-year-old mechatronics engineering student from Tanzania. I founded UMG Africa (Version Extreme Cooperation), a holding company building deep-tech that matters — from physical robotics to artificial intelligence.

I grew up in Tanzania where I saw that the biggest challenges — infrastructure, logistics, education, communication — are also the biggest engineering opportunities. Instead of waiting for solutions to come from abroad, I decided to build them myself.

My mission: build a globally influential AI, robotics, and engineering company that originates from Africa. I believe the next wave of technological revolution won't come from Silicon Valley alone — it will come from those who face the hardest problems and choose to solve them.
